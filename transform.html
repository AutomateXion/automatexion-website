One thread, from first workshop to measured benefit

Every step reads from the one before it. Nothing is retyped, so the board report, the project cases and the benefits all trace back to the assessment they came from.

01

Assess

Score the organisation against your own maturity model — any depth, your scale — and see the gap at every level. AI drafts scores from interview notes and documents, each with the quote that supports it.

02

Map processes

Who does each step, in which application, how long it takes and what goes wrong — so the roadmap stands on facts, not opinions.

03

Ideas

Turn every gap into ideas with proven methods such as SCAMPER and How Might We. AI suggests ideas per lens; the consultant keeps or drops each one.

04

Roadmap

Rate ideas on your criteria, see value against effort, and place each in a phase with an owner and months. A ranked, scheduled roadmap.

05

Strategy fit

Company strategy → goals → digital objectives → projects → outcomes, on one page. Anything the roadmap does not serve shows in red.

06

Cases

A case document per initiative: today against after for cost, people and time — so each project is approved on numbers the client recognises.

07

Board report

Where the organisation stands, the biggest gaps, the goals served, the ranked initiatives, the timeline and next steps. Built from the data, not retyped.

08

Delivery

Run the approved roadmap: health, milestones, planned against forecast, risks and issues — the steering meeting starts from one page.

09

Benefits

Every promise in a case measured month by month after go-live, set against what was promised and the company goal it counts toward.
