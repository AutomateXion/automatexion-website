Questions

Before you ask

Which channels does XionConnect support?

WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, Telegram, email and SMS come into one inbox. Ask us about any other channel you need.

Do I need a WhatsApp Business account?

Yes. XionConnect connects to WhatsApp through the official business platform, so you need a business phone number. We guide you through the setup.

Can AI reply to my customers on its own?

You decide. You can let AI answer common questions, route chats, or only suggest replies for your team. Any chat can be handed to a person at any point.

Does it work with Arabic?

Yes. Customers can write in Arabic, English or Sinhala, and your team replies in the same language.

Can it connect to my CRM or ERP?

XionConnect works with XionERP, and links to other CRM, ERP and helpdesk tools through its API and webhooks.

Where is my data stored?

We offer data residency options for the GCC and Sri Lanka. Tell us your requirements and we will confirm what fits.
