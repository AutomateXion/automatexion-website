What the company does in XionERP

Three screens under Online Store

Store Items

Choose which items show on the shop and write what each one says: name, web address, short and long description, specifications (up to 40), up to 8 pictures, HOT badge, price on enquiry, and the title and description Google shows. Filter by category, brand, stock, price and pictures, and show or hide many items at once.

Store Settings

The shop's name, web address, phone, WhatsApp, email, showroom address and hours. Also the logo and two brand colours, banners, which warehouse's stock it shows, which price list it sells at, the chat box greeting, orders on or off with delivery areas and charges, quote requests, and the About, Delivery and Returns wording.

Web Orders

New orders and quote requests. Call the shopper, then press Make sales order or Make quotation, or cancel or close with a reason.

The Online Store screens post no journal and move no stock themselves. Who can see and change them is set by user group permissions, like every other screen.

What happens to an order

From shopper to posted invoice

The shopper orders

XionERP works out the price, the delivery charge and the stock again, so the shopper cannot change a price.

The order holds its items

Straight away, so the last piece is not sold twice online.

Make sales order

The company calls the shopper and presses Make sales order: a draft from the store's warehouse, at the prices the shopper saw, with delivery as its own line.

Delivered and invoiced

As usual. The delivery note moves the stock and the invoice posts to the books.

A quote request holds nothing. Make quotation turns it into a draft quotation in Sales > Quotations. An item sold on enquiry goes on at 0, to be priced before sending.
Moving from WooCommerce

Keep your product words, pictures and Google ranking

A company leaving WordPress does not start from zero. Export the products from WordPress (Products > All Products > Export, all columns) and choose the CSV file under Import from old website.

  • Matched to your items. Each product is linked to an existing XionERP item: first where the SKU equals the item code, then where the name is exactly the same.
  • Closest matches are marked for review. Link the clear closest links the rest, only when every model number and colour in the item's name is in the old name.
  • Words and pictures copied over. Short and long description, specifications and up to 8 pictures per item, resized to store size.
  • Never touches prices or stock. It never creates an item and never changes a price or stock. Words only fill what is empty unless Replace is ticked.
  • Old web addresses redirect. Every old address permanently redirects (HTTP 308) to its new page, so Google moves its ranking to the new pages.
How it is built and kept safe

The shop never reaches your database directly

The shop is a separate website that asks XionERP for what to show.

StorefrontThe shop websiteFast on phones, close to shoppers
Shop doorA small shop doorThe shop's only way into XionERP
XionERP decidesPrice, delivery, stockWorked out again for every order

Limited questions

The shop door holds the store's key and answers only store questions: items on show, prices, stock, placing an order or a quote request.

One company's data

Every request is tied to one company. A shop sees only items that company chose to show, never cost prices, other customers or the books.

Checked pictures

Uploaded pictures are checked to be real JPEG, PNG or WebP and resized. The import fetches pictures only from the company's old website.

Only items and quantities come from the shopper. Store settings, item changes and imports are written to the audit log with who did it.

Setting up a new store

Selling within a day

A company can be selling within a day once its items and prices are in XionERP.

  1. Xion switches the module on for the company (Online Store add-on), then syncs permissions so the Online Store screens appear in the right user groups.
  2. The company fills Store Settings: name, web address, contacts, showroom hours, logo and colours, the warehouse whose stock it shows, and the price list it sells at.
  3. Moving from WooCommerce? Use Import from old website, and add the old addresses so Google follows.
  4. Choose the items on Store Items, check their words and pictures, and press Show on the store. An item needs a selling price, or Price on enquiry.
  5. Add banners and the About, Delivery and Returns wording.
  6. Switch on Orders with each delivery area and its charge, and Take quote requests if wanted.
  7. Xion puts the shop online at the company's address. Give the Web Orders screen to whoever answers orders, so new orders are called the same day.

Questions about Xion Webstore

What is Xion Webstore?

Xion Webstore gives your company its own online shop. Items, prices and stock come straight from XionERP, and web orders land as sales documents.

Do I need XionERP to use Xion Webstore?

Yes. Xion Webstore is the Online Store module for companies on XionERP and is a paid add-on. Each company gets its own shop, look and web address.

What happens when someone places an order?

A web order becomes a sales order and a quote request becomes a quotation, both in XionERP. They are then delivered, invoiced and posted the usual way.

Can I move from WordPress or WooCommerce without losing my Google ranking?

Yes. Export your products from WordPress as a CSV and import them. Product words and up to 8 pictures per item are copied over, and every old web address permanently redirects (HTTP 308) to its new page so Google moves its ranking to the new pages.

Can shoppers contact us on WhatsApp?

Yes. Every page has a WhatsApp chat box, plus Order on WhatsApp and Get a quote on WhatsApp buttons. Those chats land in your XionConnect team inbox.

Can a shopper change a price?

No. XionERP works out the price, the delivery charge and the stock again for every order, so the shopper cannot change a price.
